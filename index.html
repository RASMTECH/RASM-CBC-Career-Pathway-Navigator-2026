🧩 Recommended Grade 10 Subject Combinations

The navigator intelligently recommends Grade 10 subject combinations using the learner's strongest pathway, favourite subjects and pathway balance. When one pathway is strongest, the first two subjects are prioritised from that pathway and the third broadens the learner's options. When pathways are equal, the planner aims for one subject from each pathway. Confirm availability and final eligibility in the official Grade 10 Selection System.
