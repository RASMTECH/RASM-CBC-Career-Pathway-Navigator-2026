🧩 Recommended Grade 10 Subject Combinations

Combination rule: 2 subjects from the strongest pathway + 1 subject from one other pathway. Only this 2+1 structure is shown when one pathway is strongest.

The navigator intelligently ranks official Grade 10 subject combinations by merging the learner's interests, natural strengths, preferred activities, favourite subjects, work style, motivation, strongest pathway and the careers that best match the complete assessment. Sports & Recreation is explicitly prioritised when sports signals are strong, and security/public-service careers are included when those interests appear. Confirm availability and final eligibility in the official Grade 10 Selection System.
